
# How to Generate the IPA File

Since the command-line tools are missing the iOS 17.2 Platform files, you must use the Xcode App manually.

1. **Open the Project**
   - Go to your folder: `Desktop/Luxury-Linkage/ios/App`
   - Double-click `IMPERO DI.xcodeproj`.

2. **Select Build Target**
   - In the top-center toolbar of Xcode, click the device name (it might say "iPhone 15" or "Generic iOS Device").
   - Select **"Any iOS Device (arm64)"**.

3. **Archive**
   - In the top menu bar, click **Product**.
   - Click **Archive**.
   - Wait for the build to finish.

4. **Export IPA**
   - The "Organizer" window will appear automatically.
   - Click the blue **Distribute App** button (right side).
   - Select **TestFlight & App Store** or **App Store Connect**. Click Next.
   - Select **Export** (NOT Upload). Click Next.
   - Keep clicking Next (Accept defaults for signing).
   - Choose a location (e.g., Desktop) and click **Export**.

5. **Upload**
   - Open the folder you just saved.
   - The file `Impero Vault.ipa` (or `App.ipa`) is inside.
   - Upload this file to the Transporter app or App Store Connect.
