## 2024-05-24 - Accessibility and Focus Improvements
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons in the application layout frequently lack descriptive `aria-label`s and clear visual focus indicators, negatively impacting both screen reader and keyboard users.
**Action:** Always verify icon-only buttons include an `aria-label` and `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-primary` utility classes to ensure baseline accessibility compliance.
