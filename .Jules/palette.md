## 2024-05-10 - Adding ARIA labels and focus styles to icon-only buttons
**Learning:** Screen readers and keyboard navigation users rely on clear `aria-label`s and `focus-visible` styles to interact with icon-only buttons. Without these, they are unreadable and lack keyboard navigation context.
**Action:** Always add clear `aria-label` and `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-primary focus-visible:outline-none` classes to icon-only buttons.
